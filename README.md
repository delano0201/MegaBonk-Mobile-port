## ✨ Features

- 🎮 **Full MegaBonk gameplay** on mobile
- 📱 **Touch controls** designed specifically for phones
- 🕹️ **Virtual controls** for movement and gameplay
- ⚡ **Mobile-oriented performance optimizations**
- 🎨 **Graphics & rendering optimizations** where possible
- 🔧 **Device-specific performance tuning**
- 🖥️ **Runs without requiring a desktop PC**

> [!NOTE]
> 🔜 More optimization work is planned, and **mod support** is planned for a future release.

*The goal is to make MegaBonk playable on Android while retaining as much of the original PC experience as possible.*

---

## 📊 Performance & Device Requirements

Performance depends heavily on your device's **CPU, GPU, RAM, Android version, and thermal limits**.

| Tier | Hardware | Expected Experience |
|:---:|---|---|
| ✅ **Recommended** | Samsung Galaxy A36 5G or better | Considerably more usable — very playable, with occasional stutters |
| ⚠️ **Lower-end** | Older / budget devices | May still run, but expect the issues listed below |

<details>
<summary>📉 <b>Common issues on older or lower-end devices</b></summary>

- Low or unstable FPS
- Longer loading times
- Stuttering *(still happens on a Galaxy A36 — but it remains very playable)*
- Increased input latency
- Graphical glitches
- Crashes
- Thermal throttling
- Higher battery usage

</details>

> [!NOTE]
> The recommended hardware is **not a strict requirement** — it's simply the point where the experience is expected to become considerably more usable.

The mobile version uses **touchscreen controls** to replicate the original PC input.

- 🏃 Movement
- ⚔️ Gameplay actions
- 📋 Menus
